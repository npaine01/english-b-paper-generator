\ibtask{Your school has run a monthly repair session for a year, where
  students mend broken items brought in by people living nearby. The organisers
  have asked you to tell others about it. Write a text in which you describe how
  the sessions work, evaluate what students have gained from running them, and
  encourage more people to take part.}
  {Blog}{Email}{Social media posting}

\ibtask{Students at your school often arrive tired because lessons begin very
  early. You spoke to a sleep researcher to understand why, and you now want to
  pass on what you learned. Write a text in which you explain why sleep matters
  at your age, describe the effects of an early start, and propose changes your
  school could make.}
  {Interview}{Proposal}{Article}

\ibtask{You belong to a local walking group. A year ago your town turned a
  disused railway line into a public footpath, and your group wants to know
  whether it has been worthwhile. Write a text in which you outline the changes
  that were made, assess how well they have worked, and recommend what should
  happen next.}
  {Letter}{Official report}{Blog}
