\ibtask{You recently completed an online course that prepares you for your
  chosen field of studies. The organizers have urged you to share your
  experience with other people. Write a text in which you describe the online
  course, evaluate what you learned from it, and make a recommendation to
  others who might be interested.}
  {Email}{Interview}{Social media posting}

\ibtask{Examination anxiety causes much concern among pre-university students
  around the world. You spoke to an expert to learn more about this issue, and
  now you want to share this information with other students. Write a text in
  which you describe the common symptoms of examination anxiety, explore the
  ways this can affect students and present some techniques to reduce it.}
  {Interview}{Proposal}{Set of instructions}

\ibtask{You are a member of a local environmental organization. Six months ago
  your town introduced new actions to reduce water usage in the community, and
  your organization wants to know how successful these have been so far. Write
  a text in which you outline the actions taken, evaluate their effectiveness,
  and suggest the next steps.}
  {Letter}{Report}{Social media posting}
